Questions

Before you reach out.

Do you work with businesses outside your local area?

Yes — most support and web development work happens remotely. On-site visits are available by arrangement for network and hardware work that needs hands in the building.

What's actually included in a monthly retainer?

A pool of support hours each month (remote or on-site), a set response time, and a monthly check of backups and security settings. Anything beyond the included hours is billed at a discounted rate rather than turned away.

I already have an IT vendor or in-house person — can you still help?

Yes, this is common for web development or a specific project (a network redesign, a migration) that a generalist doesn't have time for. I'll work alongside whoever already manages your systems.

Do you sign NDAs or contracts?

Yes — a mutual NDA and a written scope of work are standard before any project starts, at no extra cost.

What if a support issue comes up outside business hours?

Retainer clients can flag anything as urgent by email or the contact form; response times outside business hours vary by plan and are confirmed when you sign up.
